Put this theme's songs in this folder with these exact file names:

  im-shipping-up-to-boston.mp3    <- I'm Shipping Up to Boston - Dropkick Murphys
  jump-around.mp3    <- Jump Around - House of Pain

Songs whose file isn't here are skipped. To add more songs, add them to
the "stpatricks" theme's tracks list in src/themes.js.
